registration opens. Registration forecasts remain in the separate history file.

https://t.me/runc_run/9303. All four calendar images were downloaded from the
official post and visually checked. The event websites still showed 2026, so
the calendar supplies a fallback until their current-season pages are updated.
The organizer explicitly says the calendar may change.

For triathlon, the official https://iron-star.com/event calendar did not list
2027 individual 1/8 in Luzhniki or Sochi/Sirius. Do not substitute Voronezh,
Zavidovo, Olympic distances, indoor races or other locations.

The official 2026 post https://t.me/ironstar_official/9129 names Luzhniki on
19 April and Sirius 1/8 on 3 October. Sirius is also confirmed by the individual
event page https://iron-star.com/results/ironstar-1-8-sirius-2026/participants
and its official competition regulations. The card therefore displays
**≈ April 2027** for Luzhniki and **≈ early October 2027** for Sirius. These are
our approximate planning periods extrapolated from one prior season, not
announced 2027 dates. No precise day is invented.

Metadata and citations are stored in `races-2027.json:race_dates` and served by
the existing worker `/status`. A current event page's date takes priority in
the card. Calendar dates never constitute proof that registration is open.
