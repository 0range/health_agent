following week. Reviewed goals: reduce fat mass by 3–4 kg while preserving strength;
October–November routine is explicitly two strength sessions plus one padel session
per week. No new calorie limit or fat-loss deadline is authorized. Keep other
health/life goals and historical plans intact; replace the obsolete primary
September goal with an audited current outcome goal, and update the upcoming
training goal with structured weekly targets.

Reuse Sunday 18:00 Moscow delivery, its existing key and bounded catch-up. Enable
the compact format by profile setting. This replaces the long automatic report
plus appended draft; `/инсайты` continues to expose the detailed breakdown. The
short report must work without an accepted weekly plan. Freeze evidence, goals,
action and text together before delivery, so retries cannot change the message.

The report covers Monday through the anchor Sunday. Sunday delivery clearly says
"на HH:MM"; only observations available by now enter the calculations. Include

training by goal-relevant type, at most one sleep/self-report observation, one
specific suggestion for next week with a reason. Aim for <=1100 characters for
the reviewed goals; cap user-facing goal labels. Distinguish observations from
causal conclusions and unrecorded workouts from skipped workouts.

reports, deduplicated by day/type. Running/swimming do not count as strength;
generic racket sport does not prove padel. Negative/planned manual text must not
count as performed. Keep unknown types visible. Current phase goals determine
evaluation; next-week goals determine the suggestion.

when those sessions are not confirmed; address missing measurements or incomplete
food records when they block the body goal; test a supported dinner-timing pattern
only as a hypothesis; otherwise continue the working routine and recheck it. No
automatic calorie restriction, load escalation or arbitrary new diet rules.
Saved goals and new proposals must express strength/padel separately; accepting a
plan remains explicit. The compact report itself is advice, not an accepted plan.

Validation: fixed week boundaries, Sunday weigh-in, goals at month transition,
units/sparse data, workout type/profile isolation, one action, replay, scheduled
delivery without accepted plan and original delivery key. Private preview uses
real records; no manually sent Telegram test message or synthetic health records.
