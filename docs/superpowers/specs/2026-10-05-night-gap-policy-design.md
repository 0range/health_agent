# Nightly analysis: ten-minute gap allowance

The user approved this rule on 5 October: retain the night of 13–14 September;
exclude a night for missing data only when its longest continuous gap exceeds
10 minutes. Exactly 600 seconds is accepted. Coverage percentages and all observed
gaps remain visible; there is no separate 99% coverage exclusion in the analysis.

Use the same rule for required air, CO₂ and HR series within the actual sleep
interval, including leading and trailing gaps. Empty series cannot be compared.
Location, bedroom placement, scored WHOOP sleep and provisional-current-night
rules remain applicable. Analysis scope starts with bedroom observations on
10 September; no earlier WHOOP history is used for study conclusions.

Operational collection checks retain their >=99% / <=60-second warning criteria.
An operational warning is not an instruction to exclude a night from research.
The shared analysis helper, research protocol and runbook make this explicit.

Preserve the prior private review before regenerating its statistics, CSV, report
and charts. Record this as a user-directed amendment after viewing initial results,
not as a prospectively chosen rule. Keep observations unchanged and do not fill gaps.
Boundary tests cover 66, 600 and 606 seconds, low total coverage, empty data and
leading/trailing gaps. Verify the real night is retained for both participants.
