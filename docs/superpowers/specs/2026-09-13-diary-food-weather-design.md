# Structured sleep diary, food continuity, and outdoor weather

User-requested scope: collect hourly weather context; morning sleep questionnaire at 08:00 Moscow; fix food replies/reminders, distinguish carbohydrate sources, and give one actionable weekly change. Existing user authorization covers implementation and live corrections; no extra approval gate.

## Sleep
Use the existing morning delivery/receipt mechanism, default 08:00 and update the owner's persisted schedule. Two subjective questions only: overall last-night sleep quality 0–10 and feeling rested on awakening 0–10. No latency, awakening counts or minute estimates. Each prompt has a Telegram reply keyboard with 0–10 and skip; the next question appears after one tap, the keyboard is removed on completion. /опрос starts or resumes today's questionnaire. Draft and answers persist by profile and Moscow wake date; duplicate taps cannot advance the wrong question or overwrite completed answers. Unknown/skip remains null. Optional /сон free-text comments stay supported without prompting for them. Preserve response timestamps, schema_version=2 and original user turns. This is a short Russian adaptation, not a validated SQS/RSQ score; do not equate self-report with WHOOP Recovery or assume it is a proxy for PSG.

Evidence checked from original article abstracts via Europe PMC: Snyder et al., 2018, doi:10.5664/jcsm.7478 (single-item scale validity relative to other subjective questionnaires in insomnia/depression); Drake et al., 2014, doi:10.5664/jcsm.3860 (restorative-sleep measure distinguishes complaints even without PSG onset/maintenance abnormalities). Correlation with questionnaires is not validation against objective sleep. Use two items to reduce burden and avoid overlapping alertness/rested questions.

## Food
Reproduce actual failures: a food description after breakfast notice is a meal without an extra 'new' confirmation; 'Покушал...' after the next-meal notice starts a new meal. Explicit additions/corrections remain attached; plans, refusals and questions must not become eaten meals. Preserve receipt idempotency and original event times. Show the same reminder target that due() uses, including snoozes; keep the existing explicit rejection of snoozes outside the permitted window. Repair today's misattached lunch with a private audit backup and reanalyse both meals from their own evidence. Never resend historic notices.

Classify named carbohydrate sources conservatively: whole grains/legumes (complex, fiber-rich), refined starches, added/free sugars, whole fruit, unknown. Do not equate all starch with slow digestion or infer sugar grams/GI from a photo. Store these categories with ingredient evidence and include concise relevant feedback.

Weekly message: one concrete action plus one factual basis from logged meals, at most 450 characters. Deterministic grounded selection avoids verbose provider fallback and unsupported claims; missing logs do not mean missing meals.

## Weather
One shared location, the configured study district, approximate district coordinates held only in private config. Open-Meteo hourly model data with source URL, retrieval time, model-data label, units and original responses. Fields: temperature, relative humidity, dew point, mean sea-level/surface pressure, wind speed/direction/gusts, precipitation, cloud cover. Use recent historical forecast series, never label them station observations. Fetch past study days daily, exclude future timestamps, idempotently update hourly records while retaining immutable raw response hashes. Unknown remains null, not zero. Hourly source rows live beside study data in PostgreSQL and daily weather CSV exports; no fabricated 6-second weather. Existing hourly quality job performs a once-daily weather sync and checks previous-day 24-hour/field coverage; failure is visible and does not block WHOOP/air collection.

## Verification and rollout
Regression tests for actual food conversation, corrections, denied/planned meals, retries, scheduling and snooze; sleep numeric validation, profile separation and restart; weather timestamp/units/null/coverage/replay using mocked HTTP. Run affected suites and static checks. Restart main/food bots after implementation, inspect poll health, run live weather backfill and QC, and inspect durable schedule/records. Push code/docs only; private data stays ignored.
