Health Agent

A private, local-first assistant that helps its users collect and understand their own health history.

What it does

  • Imports health documents from folders a user explicitly selects.
  • Reads new health-related email and wearable data after the user grants access.
  • Organizes confirmed laboratory results in a private spreadsheet and dashboard.
  • Answers personal questions through a private Telegram bot.

Local by default

The application runs on the user's own computer. Each person connects their own accounts, and their records are kept in a separate local profile. Google Drive and Gmail access is read-only; spreadsheet access is limited to files created by Health Agent.

User control

Connections can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account permissions. Local records can be removed directly by the user. Health Agent is not a medical provider and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.
