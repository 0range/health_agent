# Privacy Policy — Vitali Health Agent

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Vitali Health Agent is a private, locally operated personal-health application.
It accesses a WHOOP account only after the member grants access through WHOOP's
official OAuth flow.

## Data used

The app may read the WHOOP profile, body measurements, cycles, recovery, sleep,
and workouts authorized by the member. It stores source responses and derived
health metrics on the owner's Mac so the owner can review personal trends.

## Data handling

- WHOOP passwords are never collected.
- OAuth credentials and health data are stored locally and are not committed to
  this GitHub repository.
- Personal data is not sold, advertised against, or shared with data brokers.
- Data is sent to another service only when the owner explicitly enables that
  service for a requested Health Agent feature.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on the local Mac until the owner deletes the profile or local data.
WHOOP access can be revoked from the WHOOP account or by disconnecting the local
connector. Revocation stops future synchronization; the owner can then delete
the retained local copy.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository without including
credentials, medical records, or other sensitive personal data.
